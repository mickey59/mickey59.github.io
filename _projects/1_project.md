The below teaching material was created with the aim in mind to create engaging and informative material that explained to graduate engineers the importance of sustainability as a global collective and how robotic systems can be leveraged to help development.

In the video I introduce the two main classes of robotic systems Service and Industrial robots. I also discuss the United Nations Standard Development Goals, and finish by focusing on a specific use case where robotic systems can be especially useful.
